the story

R&B, Soul and everything
in between.

El Lambert's journey is as diverse as his talents. Hailing from Martinsburg, West Virginia, his musical roots run deep, shaped by the soul-stirring sounds of R&B, Soul, Neo-Soul, Gospel and Jazz. His journey took flight in the D.C. music scene from 2013–2017, before finding a new home in Charlotte, North Carolina.

  1. 01

    The Performer

    As an entertainer and event host, El brings more than musical talent to the stage — he offers an unforgettable experience. R&B grooves, soulful melodies, Neo-Soul vibes, Gospel energy and Jazz improvisation blend into a unique sonic adventure that moves audiences and creates memories, whether the room is intimate or a large crowd.

  2. 02

    The Ensemble

    Book El solo, with The El Lambert Band (1–7 pieces), or bring the full El Lambert R&B Party Band (7–13 pieces) for a night that fills the floor. Every configuration is curated to the room and the occasion.

  3. 03

    The Consultant

    Beyond the stage, El has over 10 years of experience as a marketing consultant — developing impactful strategies that help businesses grow, sharpen their brand presence, and increase revenue. That same precision and creativity shows up in every project and partnership.
