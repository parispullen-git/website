Background

Welcome to El Lambert's Funeral & Religious Services, where music meets solace in moments of remembrance and worship. With an unwavering commitment to providing unparalleled musical support, El brings a wealth of experience to funeral homes, churches and events. Having lent his musical talents to several hundred funerals and religious services as both a seasoned musician and vocalist, El understands the importance of creating an atmosphere of comfort and reflection during these sensitive occasions.

Why Choose El Lambert

Experience. A rich background spanning numerous funerals and religious services brings a depth of understanding to the nuances of creating a meaningful and respectful musical ambiance.

Versatility. A masterful musician and vocalist, El is adept in various genres, specializing in Traditional and Contemporary Gospel, R&B and Soul — a customized, emotionally impactful experience for diverse audiences.

Professionalism. Seamless coordination with funeral directors, clergy and event organizers ensures a harmonious collaboration, so you can focus on providing compassionate support to grieving families.

Heartfelt testimonials. Previous clients have praised El's ability to infuse grace and compassion into every performance, turning moments of sorrow into beautiful memories.
