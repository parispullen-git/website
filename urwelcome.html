The Composition

Three movements.

Built to arrive quietly and stay a long time. The opening is the shortest part of it and the part almost nobody remembers — which is the point.

Top

Bergamot · Black pepper · Pink grapefruit

The first ten minutes. Bright, dry, slightly sharp — a handshake rather than an introduction.

Heart

Leather · Tobacco flower · Cedar

Where it settles, about an hour in. This is the part people mean when they ask what you are wearing.

Base

Oud · Vanilla · Amber

What stays on a collar for two days. Warm, close, and slightly sweet under the smoke.

The Making

Mixed by hand.
Badly, at first.

It began on a kitchen table with a set of small scales, a rack of blotters, and a room that smelled like a chemistry set for eleven months.

The base was reformulated three times after it was already selling, on the grounds that selling and finished are different words. Nothing about that was efficient. All of it was necessary.

There was never an advertisement. People stood close, asked what he was wearing, and he told them the name and then said nothing else at all. That silence did more work than a campaign would have.
