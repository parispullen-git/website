the story

Great music is not
simply heard.

Three Piece Entertainment is the live-experience company founded by Harvey Cummings II — saxophonist, pianist, producer, and cultural curator whose work has been part of moments connected to President Barack Obama, Oprah Winfrey, Michael Jordan, the Carolina Panthers, and Super Bowl programming.

  1. 01

    What we build

    Elevated live music experiences, cultural programming, concert productions, and signature events that celebrate culture and inspire connection — designed and produced end to end.

  2. 02

    Who we build it for

    From Fortune 500 receptions and luxury weddings to sold-out theaters and intimate private gatherings. Corporations, municipalities, museums, universities, hospitality groups, nonprofits, festivals, planners, and agencies.

  3. 03

    Why it lands

    Every engagement is built on one belief: great music is not simply heard — it is felt, remembered, and carried forward. Intentional, community-centered, and operationally excellent, every time.
