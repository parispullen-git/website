01 The Experience

The Partnership

The BLK Girl BLK Dress Dinner Party is more than a dinner. It is an experience centred on Black women feeling celebrated in the room. Because music shapes so much of that energy, this is written as a partnership rather than a booking.

The four hours have a shape: arrival and welcome, dinner and programme, and a back half where the room opens up. Each option below covers the full window and changes how much live music carries it.
