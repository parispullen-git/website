01 — The Story

A tradition spoken
with a modern accent.

Harvey Cummings II is a saxophonist, pianist, producer, composer, educator and cultural curator whose work lives at the intersection of music, storytelling, culture, and unforgettable live experiences.

  1. 01

    Roots

    A Charlotte native and graduate of North Carolina Central University. His debut Chicken Day EP takes its name from the weekly cafeteria ritual that turned an HBCU campus into a congregation — a record about where he is from, and who raised him.

  2. 02

    Range

    Keys and saxophone alongside 9th Wonder for Cartoon Network's The Boondocks. Stages shared with Anthony Hamilton, Angie Stone, Giveon, TLC and Fantasia. A performance for President Barack Obama. Programming for the Carolina Panthers and Super Bowl weekend.

  3. 03

    Language

    Gospel discipline, bebop grit, hip-hop energy. Music that moves from a hymn to a street anthem without dropping a beat — high-art sensibility carried on an R&B cadence, built to bring a younger and wider audience into the room.

Jazz is Black American music gone worldwide.
Jazz is the granddaddy.

      05 — Legacy

      The music only
      survives if it is handed down.

      Mentorship, masterclasses, school workshops and community partnerships sit at the centre of Harvey's practice — not beside it. The work is preservation of a Black music legacy, and the deliberate business of making that legacy reachable for the next player coming up.

      • Masterclasses & clinicsInstrument, improvisation and the professional realities of a working musician's career.
      • School & youth programsEnsemble coaching and performance residencies with schools and community organisations.
      • Artist mentorshipOne-to-one development for emerging Charlotte musicians, on and off the stand.
