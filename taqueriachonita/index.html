Taqueria Chonita opened on Freedom Dr in 2021 as a family operation — small counter, small menu, everything made fresh when you order it. No reservations, no waitstaff, just real Mexican street food for West Charlotte done the way it's supposed to be done.

You won't find us posting fancy plating on Instagram. Find us on Yelp, find us on Freedom Dr, and find out why the regulars keep coming back for the same order every time.
