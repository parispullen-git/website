The building

house.html is generated from a Python data structure in build_house.py. Never hand-edit it. Sixteen floors, fifty-seven artifacts.

dict(id="bar", lvl="02", name="The Bar", state="public", img="env-bar", grade="", focus="46% 54%", note="Low ceiling, low light, low voices…", arts=[("ice","The Ice","36%","64%","Cut from a single block…", [("Cut","Daily")])])

PieceClassBehaviour
Floor scene.floor-sceneFull-viewport room. --focus sets object-position, --zoom the scale.
Grades.grade--warm / noir / cold / dawn / amber / jewelLets one photograph serve several rooms
Artifact.artifactHotspot at --x/--y. Pulsing brass dot, label on hover.
Drawer.drawerOne panel at a time. Esc closes. Leaving the floor closes it.
Artifact positions are element-relative, not image-relative. Because floors use object-fit: cover, hotspots drift slightly against the underlying object as viewport aspect changes. Treat them as regions of interest, not pixel pins.

07

Voice

Rules

Short statements with double meanings. Never explain the joke.
Specific numbers beat adjectives. "Twenty-four bays", not "many cars".
Understate the flex. The rail is polished weekly because a rail with no wear means nobody is standing here.
Never corporate, motivational-speaker, or hustle language.
Admit the cost. The dossier names the open ledger. That is what makes the rest credible.

Canonical lines

The city thinks he’s selling luxury. The people who matter know he’s selling access.
Everyone is welcome. Not everyone gets access.
Presence arrives before introduction.
Information has a way of finding the right room.
Your measurements are held under a record number, not your name.
Nothing is charged today. The fee is settled at the fitting.

Last two recovered from the previous build — retained, they are the best commerce copy we have.

The Vault & the Wall

UR Welcome — the vault

A node on the bottle opens a four-step panel: intro → product → reserve list → thank you. The product is sold out and the buy button is deliberately inert; the path forward is the list.

Where the details actually go. A static site stores nothing. assets/js/vault.js opens with window.UR_WELCOME_ENDPOINT. Paste a Formspree / Netlify / Basin endpoint and submissions POST there with a silent confirmation. Leave it empty and the form falls back to opening a pre-addressed email — which works, but the thank-you then says so honestly rather than claiming a signup that did not happen. Never change that copy to imply storage that is not configured.
PieceBehaviour
.vault__nodeTwo offset pings plus a core, pinned to the bottle at 50% / 53%
.vault__stepOne visible at a time, switched by data-goto
.vault__soldoutThe honest state. .vault__buy is styled disabled and carries aria-disabled.
.vault__noteThe personal message from Paris, signed. Shown only after a successful submit, with the first name interpolated.

The Firm — the studio wall

Client work pinned to a wall. Each .pin sits at --x/--y, pulses on its own --i offset, names the brand on hover and opens that brand’s file in casefiles.html, generated by build_casefiles.py.

Case files state where each brand actually stands — including “opened twice, no reply yet” and “not yet presented”. That candour is what makes the live ones credible. Do not sand it off.

The Drafting Room — hidden floor

Level 19. A private index of speculative work: sites built for a brand before they have agreed to buy. Generated by build_drafting.py. Not on the elevator panel, not in the directory, not linked from any public page, and noindex.

PieceDetail
Indexdrafting/_index-private-<suffix>.html — pipeline table with status, ask, code and expiry
Proposaldrafting/<slug>-<hex>.html — one per brand, access-code gated, Paris Pullen LLC lockup
Statusdraft · sent · viewed · won · passed — each with its own colour
Pricing$1,500 build (one time) · $50/mo care — 2 updates, hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring
CountdownSticky bar to the expiry date. Turns brass under 72 hours, red on expiry.
Exit intentFires on cursor leaving through the top — but only after the reader has engaged (a real mousemove or scroll) AND six seconds have passed. Back-button fallback on touch. Once per session.
StripePayment Link per commission. Empty = button inert and the client is told an invoice follows. Never ship with a dead link.
DataAll commissions live in drafting_data.py; the builder only renders
Rotate URLsChange SUFFIX / hexid in the data file and re-run
The unlisted URL is the only real boundary. Access codes are presentation and friction — the code sits in the page source, so anyone with the link and a devtools window can read it. Never put payment details, contracts or client data on this floor. Anything genuinely confidential needs a server with real authentication.

The City Guide

A full-bleed interactive map surface: the aerial plate with a survey overlay pinned to it and a HUD card reading out the selected district.

The balcony on the homepage is Charlotte, rendered as thirteen districts over the skyline. Districts are data, not markup — edit assets/js/city.js and the SVG rebuilds itself over the aerial plate.

{ id:'noda', name:'NoDa', x:626, y:208, rx:70, ry:46, rot:14, kind:'Arts district', note:'Murals, live rooms and the best crowd in the city…', best:'Late', dress:'Yourself, but pressed', venues:[ { name:'…', kind:'Bar' } ] }

PieceBehaviour
.cm-zoneOne district. Hover, focus or click lights it brass and paints the reading panel. Keyboard reachable.
.cityreadThe panel: kind, name, note, best/dress/listed, then venues. Falls back to “Venues being compiled” while venues is empty.
.citymap-wrap::beforeA radial well behind the map so districts read over a bright skyline
PositionsPinned to the aerial plate in its own 2000×849 coordinate space, so the overlay lines up with the photograph at any width. Placement is compositional — districts sit where they read in the frame, not where a survey would put them.
MarkersAlways-visible brass dot and halo; on hover a soft radial lights the district, a dashed ring appears, four reticle brackets snap around it and the label brightens
SurfaceFull-bleed, aspect-locked to the plate (2800×1188). Under 980px it pans sideways rather than squashing.
Blueprint chromeSurvey grid, edge ticks, corner brackets, compass, scale bar and a slow scan sweep — all drawn in chrome(), all brass, no sci-fi glow
To add a venue, push { name, kind } onto that district’s venues array. The count in the panel updates itself.

The Museum

Client work on the homepage, shown as exhibits. .ba is a before/after wipe — drag, or arrow-key when focused.

Every current exhibit is “built from nothing” — none of the four clients had a prior site, so the before panel shows a hatched plate rather than a fabricated “bad old website”. When a genuine before exists, drop it in as .ba__before and remove the .ba__none plate.

The Drafting Room desk

A local macOS app at /Applications/Drafting Room.app, source in /Volumes/HQ/Claude/drafting-room/. The pipeline is rendered as floors of The Compliment — a client sits on the floor matching their stage, and each brand has a locker there.

StageFloorRoom
MockupB2Inventory
Proposal19Drafting Room
Pitch02The Bar
Closing03The Restaurant
OnboardingGThe Lobby
In Build11Fitting Floor
Live29Skyline Club

It pulls world.css and the Foxx mark from this project at runtime, so the desk cannot drift from the design system. Data is data/clients.json, written atomically with forty rolling backups.

Not a CRM platform. No email or SMS sending, no calendar sync, no payments, no automation, no multi-user. It binds to localhost with no authentication — never store card numbers, passwords or signed contracts in it.
