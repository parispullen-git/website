On Beatties Ford Rd

Part of a corner with deep roots

Beatties Ford Rd has been the heartbeat of West Charlotte's Black community for generations — churches, barbershops, and family kitchens that have fed this neighborhood for decades. Sunset Soulfood belongs to that same corner and that same tradition: home-style cooking, made the way it's always been made, for the people who live here.

No frills, no gimmicks — just a kitchen that takes Sunday dinner seriously, seven days a week except Monday.
