The Craft

Where the
brim is born

The signature Burns Brims finish begins with fire. Flame is drawn across raw felt to open the nap, char the edge and force the material to take on a character no factory press can imitate. What comes out the other side is scorched, shaped and singular — the reason the collection is called Fire Crowns.

Custom Design

How it works

Three steps from a conversation to a hat that only exists because you asked for it.

01

Consultation

Book a session and we talk it through — your style, the occasion, what you want the hat to say before you open your mouth. Nothing is drawn until we understand that.

02

Design & Creation

Marquise builds a design proposal from your brief. Once you approve it, traditional hat-making technique and the studio’s burn-and-block finish turn it into a physical object.

03

Delivery & Unveiling

Choose secure shipping — or the Full Brim Service, where Marquise flies out and hands it to you himself. Aftercare, maintenance and repair are included for the life of the hat.

The Maker

“I love bringing creations to life that make people feel good and confident. I am truly living in my purpose.”

— Marquise “Hollywood” Burns, Founder

Marquise founded Burns Brims on a commitment to use his creative talent to make a positive impact. The studio backs community initiatives — particularly mental health work in the Black community — because a hat should mean something beyond the person wearing it. Intentional action today, a better tomorrow. One brim at a time.
