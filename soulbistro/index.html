The story

Started by someone who left a desk job to cook

Soul Bistro launched in 2021, started by a retired telecom professional who traded a desk for a smoker. What began as a single food trailer has grown into a full catering operation — same Southern comfort food, now serving private parties and tailgates across Charlotte alongside the truck's regular stops.
