STOP 01 / LOBBY

Level 01

The Lobby

The room that reads you first.

Marble, dark walnut, brass, water and flowers cut at a scale that tells you the budget was never the constraint.

Nobody is turned away at the door. That would be crude, and it would be bad business. The sorting happens later, quietly, in the direction the concierge points you. Ask the concierge.

Concierge protocol

Three questions, none of them intrusive: who are you here to see, how long are you staying, and what would make the evening worth it. The answers decide which elevator you are shown to. Most guests never learn there are two.

STOP 02 / SUITES

Levels 04–22

The Suites

Rooms that keep what they hear.

Deep tubs, heavy curtains, a desk built for actual work, and a door that closes with the weight of a bank vault.

A suite is not a bedroom with a better view. It is a private office you happen to be able to sleep in — which is exactly how the people who book them use them.

01The CornerLight

Two walls of glass, morning on one side, the skyline on the other. Booked by people who take calls before six.

02The StudyFocus

Panelled, low-lit, no television. There is a decanter and a good chair and nothing else asking for your attention.

03The ResidencePermanence

A kitchen, a dressing room, a second entrance. For guests whose stay is measured in seasons.

STOP 03 / PRIVATE FLOOR

Unlisted

The Private Floor

Every room has a door.

Not every door is for everyone.

It does not appear on the elevator panel. It is not on the floor plan the fire marshal has, and yes, that is a joke. There is a library, a long table, six chairs, and a rule that nothing said at that table gets repeated at any other one.

The right people always know.

Membership is not applied for. It is noticed. Somebody already at the table says a name, the table agrees, and an invitation arrives with no explanation of how it was decided. The absence of a process is the process.

STOP 04 / FOXX MOTOR CLUB

Below Grade

Foxx Motor Club

Machines
with manners.

Automotive culture for people who understand the difference between having a car and having a presence.

Glass walls, polished concrete, a lounge at the far end and a private elevator that goes up to a floor most guests do not know exists. The cars are lit like sculpture because that is what they are.

STOP 05 / THE ROOFTOP

Level 29

The Rooftop

Everybody
wants in.

Infinity pool, private dining, cabanas, a sound system somebody actually paid for, and a view that makes people honest.

Information has a way of finding the right room.

You do not need to be the loudest man up here. Just the one everyone remembers on Monday.
