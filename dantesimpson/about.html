Dr. Dante Simpson, Co-Founder & Former CMO, ESPAT

Dante Simpson has spent two decades moving deliberately between industries that shape culture — and building the connective tissue between them.

An Ohio State University graduate, his path runs through Gucci Group under Tom Ford, Sony BMG, and a stint on the trading floor at the New York Stock Exchange — stops that, by his own account in interviews and profile materials, shaped an eye for brand, an ear for talent, and a discipline for capital that would later define ESPAT. As pocstock put it on naming him to its advisory board, he is "a creative powerhouse whose journey spans fashion, music, gaming, media, finance, and the evolving world of AI." (Employment history is drawn from Simpson's own interviews and public profile and is presented here as reported, pending independent verification.)

In 2019 he co-founded ESPAT Studios with Ed Brooks and Mario Prosperino, setting out to build "a home for premium creatives with a commitment to gaming culture" — a bet that gaming, music, fashion, art, technology and sports were converging into a single audience worth building for. That bet expanded into ESPAT TV, a production and distribution studio, and ESPAT Labs, the ecosystem's AI and technology arm, with Simpson serving as Co-Founder and CMO across the ecosystem through its February 2026 Backboard.io partnership. ESPAT has since been sold, and Simpson is no longer affiliated with its current operations.

He has become a recurring voice on gaming, representation and the business of culture — speaking at GDC's "Play Beyond the Game," the BET Experience gaming panel, and a SXSW-adjacent "Future-Proofing Diversity in Gaming" session. He also holds board and advisory roles across pocstock, Path Intelligence, MMCA, and Benson Petroleum.

Since 2024 he has also hosted The Table, a FINTECH.TV interview show taped live on the New York Stock Exchange floor — sitting down with founders, executives and entertainers including Hannah Bronfman, Mario Van Peebles, Kenya Moore, and Earn Your Leisure's Troy Millings and Rashad Bilal.

A note on claims in circulation: Public and promotional materials describe ESPAT as the country's top producer of video-game trailers, holder of gaming-ad rights across roughly 95% of U.S. movie screens, the first Black-owned studio to produce a Super Bowl broadcast, a contributor to franchises including Halo, Call of Duty, Fortnite and Grand Theft Auto, and a partner on NFL animated simulcasts for ESPN — alongside a 2025 pocstock/NYSE recognition citing a "$175 million" build over five years. These are directionally relevant to ESPAT's positioning but originate from interview, social and promotional sources rather than independent trade reporting or audited financials, and are presented here as reported rather than verified. Separately, a Crunchbase listing describes a "Dante Simpson" as Chief Marketing Officer of ESPAT and an advisory board member of Benson Petroleum — both confirmed accurate. CMO, not CEO, is the correct title for Simpson's ESPAT tenure, and it's reflected throughout this site. Several partner press releases and interviews from 2021–2026 referred to him as "CEO" of ESPAT TV at the time; those are quoted here as originally published, though CMO is the accurate title. ESPAT has since been sold, and Simpson holds no current role or ownership in the company.
Timeline

The build, year by year.

  • 2019 — Origin
    ESPAT Studios founded with co-founders Ed Brooks and Mario Prosperino, positioned as a creative collective at the intersection of gaming culture, music, fashion, art, tech and sports.
  • 2021 — Enthusiast Gaming
    ESPAT signs an integrated content partnership pairing premium gaming programming with Enthusiast Gaming's fan-community distribution, working alongside Ridley Scott Creative Group, Petrol, PRG and Movers & Shakers.
  • 2021 — Screenvision Media
    A partnership to produce and distribute gaming content — primarily AAA trailers — across cinema and sports out-of-home advertising networks.
  • 2022 — Pod Digital Media
    Pod Digital Media secures advertising rights tied to ESPAT TV, extending the studio's reach into commercials and brand-integrated content.
  • 2024 — The Table
    Simpson begins hosting The Table for FINTECH.TV, live from the NYSE floor — ongoing conversations with founders, executives and entertainers.
  • 2025 — Board & Advisory Roles
    Simpson joins the advisory board of pocstock, with a remit spanning creative, cultural, gaming, media, finance and AI strategy — alongside board and advisory roles with Path Intelligence, MMCA, and Benson Petroleum.
  • 2026 — ESPAT Labs & Backboard.io
    ESPAT and Backboard.io announce a strategic AI-infrastructure partnership focused on memory-first creator and audience tooling. ESPAT is later sold; Simpson's tenure as Co-Founder and CMO of ESPAT concludes.
