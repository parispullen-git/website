Case Study 02 — Creative Collective

ESPAT Studios

The Challenge

Talented creatives were scattered across industries that didn't talk to each other.

The best people working in gaming culture weren't necessarily gaming-industry veterans — they were coming out of fashion, music, art and sports, each with their own networks, agencies and production houses. No single studio had assembled a bench deep enough, or credible enough, to serve premium gaming and entertainment briefs at the level brands like the NFL, PlayStation or major game publishers expected.

The Approach

Build a collective, not a roster.

ESPAT Studios positioned itself as a convergence platform rather than a traditional agency — pulling in production, animation and creative organizations with decades of combined pedigree, and giving them a shared home built specifically around gaming culture. The result draws on partners including Ridley Scott Creative Group, Petrol, PRG, Zoic and The Famous Group, with producers, directors and creative partners based across New York, Los Angeles, Minneapolis, London, Amsterdam and Madrid, while maintaining dedicated studios in New York and Los Angeles.

The output spans the range gaming and entertainment briefs actually require — esports reels, animation and creature-creation work, branded gaming content, and large-scale activations like NFL mosaic and virtual fan experiences.

The Results

Credits across the biggest names in gaming and entertainment.

ESPAT Studios' public reel credits work spanning major publishers and platforms — including PlayStation, Marvel's Avengers, Tom Clancy's Elite Squad, JBL Quantum gaming hardware, and NFL and Boston Celtics activations — produced through the collective's partner network rather than a single in-house crew.
